GLAZING & FACADE FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers regarding our Frederick glass fabrication capabilities, curtain wall systems, ASTM water testing, and Mid-Atlantic service area.

Does ECP fabricate and pre-glaze commercial glass systems in-house?
Yes. At our Frederick, Maryland facility (5422 Mount Zion Rd), we perform multi-axis CNC aluminum machining, curtain wall and storefront frame assembly, custom historic window sash replication, and controlled shop pre-glazing. We then deliver sequenced units directly to your jobsite on our own USDOT-registered fleet (#897979).
Which architectural glass and aluminum framing manufacturers does ECP partner with?
We work directly with the industry's leading architectural aluminum and glass manufacturers, including Kawneer, YKK AP, EFCO, Oldcastle BuildingEnvelope (OBE), Viracon, Vitro Architectural Glass, and Guardian Glass, allowing us to match virtually any basis-of-design specification or propose cost-saving value-engineering alternates.
How does ECP outperform traditional glazing-only subcontractors?
Unlike glazing-only subcontractors who stop at the aluminum frame perimeter, ECP is also a licensed structural engineering and general contracting firm (51+ years in business). We can engineer, frame, and flash the structural rough opening AND fabricate/install the curtain wall or storefront, eliminating trade finger-pointing, slab-edge conflicts, and water-leak disputes.
Do you perform ASTM / AAMA water infiltration testing and PE-stamped shop drawings?
Yes. Our in-house CAD/BIM drafting and engineering team produces full Division 08 shop drawings and PE-stamped wind-load, dead-load, and blast calculations. We also conduct shop mockup and field ASTM E1105 / AAMA 501.1 & 501.2 water filtration testing to certify watertight building envelopes.
What geographic territory do your glazing crews serve?
From our Frederick, MD headquarters along the I-270/I-70 corridor, our merit-shop glazing crews and delivery fleet actively serve all of Maryland (Baltimore, Montgomery, PG, Frederick, and Eastern/Western MD), Washington, DC, Northern Virginia, and West Virginia.
Is ECP fully bonded and insured for large commercial facade packages?
Yes. ECP carries comprehensive general liability, workers' compensation, commercial fleet (USDOT #897979), and professional liability insurance, backed by strong bonding capacity and a 93/100 BuildZoom rating (top 27% of Maryland contractors).
