OUR STORY & FACILITY

Why we engineer and fabricate our own glazing systems.

Engineered Construction Products, Ltd. was founded in Frederick, MD in 1975 on a simple premise: a building's glass envelope and its structural framing should never be treated as disconnected trades. For more than 51 years, we have engineered, fabricated, and installed commercial glazing and structural systems that stand up to the Mid-Atlantic's most demanding architectural specifications.

We don't just broker glass, we engineer the curtain wall mullions, CNC-fabricate and pre-glaze the frames in our Frederick facility, water-test the assembly, and install it with our own field crews.

Under the leadership of President Kurtis Barron, ECP partners directly with premier architectural glass and framing manufacturers, including Kawneer, YKK AP, EFCO, Viracon, Oldcastle BuildingEnvelope, Vitro, and Guardian Glass. Because we also hold active General Contracting and Structural Engineering capabilities, we resolve slab-edge, rough-opening, and anchorage challenges weeks before a typical glazing subcontractor even steps onto the jobsite.
