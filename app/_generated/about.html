OUR STORY

Engineering knowledge in every component we supply and build.

Engineered Construction Products, Ltd. was founded in 1975 with a clear purpose: bring true engineering discipline to specialty construction products and field execution across the Mid-Atlantic region. For over five decades, we have honored that commitment.

We don't just supply products — we bring the engineering knowledge to ensure every component performs exactly as designed, for the life of the structure.

Under the leadership of President Kurtis Barron, ECP serves general contractors, developers, architects, and building owners who value a partner with real technical depth. Our team combines hands-on field experience with an engineering mindset — solving constructability and supply challenges before they become cost overruns.
